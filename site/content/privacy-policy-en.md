# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 1, 2026

## Overview

Plotree is a desktop application for designing story plots using flowchart-style node editors. This privacy policy explains how Plotree handles your data.

## Data Collection

**Plotree does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** The application operates entirely offline on your local machine.

Specifically:

- **No network communication:** Plotree does not connect to the internet, send analytics, or make any network requests.
- **No personal information:** Plotree does not collect names, email addresses, usage data, or any other personally identifiable information.
- **No telemetry:** Plotree does not include any telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking features.

## Data Storage

All data created by Plotree (`.plotree` project files) is stored locally on your device in the locations you choose. Plotree does not upload or sync your files to any cloud service.

## Third-Party Services

Plotree does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Htkym/Plotree).
